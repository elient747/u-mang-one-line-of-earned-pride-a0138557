One original line, 1 to 240 characters: a quiet, earned moment of pride — after eleven years of night shifts, nurse Mara watches the premature baby she fed drop by drop walk out of the ward on his own two feet.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```

Writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
